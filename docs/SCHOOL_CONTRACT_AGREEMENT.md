## 1. RECITALS & OBJECTIVE

WHEREAS:
- The Service Provider owns and operates an AI-driven school operations and academic scheduling platform known as **"AI Smart Calendar"** (hosted at `https://smartcalender.learnx.school` and associated mobile/web endpoints).
- The School desires to subscribe to and utilize the AI Smart Calendar SaaS platform for automated timetable generation, dynamic substitution management, biometric attendance integration, teacher leave workflows, and school scheduling.
- The Parties wish to define the terms, conditions, pricing, data privacy, and service levels governing this deployment.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES

The Service Provider will provide the School with access to the cloud-based **AI Smart Calendar Enterprise Suite**, which includes:

1. **AI-Powered Timetable Studio & Optimization Engine**:
   - Automatic generation of conflict-free class, teacher, and room timetables.
   - Support for curriculum constraints (CBSE, ICSE, IB, Cambridge, State Boards).
   - Period distribution limits, consecutive lab/double-period handling, and teacher workload balancing.

2. **Dynamic Live Substitutions**:
   - One-click and automated AI substitution suggestions based on teacher absence and subject specialization.
   - Instant WhatsApp, SMS, and Email alerts to substitute faculty.

3. **Faculty Directory & Leave Management**:
   - Teacher profile records, working hour quotas, and department mappings.
   - Self-service leave requests and multi-tier approval workflows.

4. **Biometric & Attendance Integration**:
   - Integration with biometric hardware (ZKTeco/Essl/Matrix/API logs) for automatic staff punch-in/out tracking.

5. **Room, Lab & Asset Allocation**:
   - Conflict-free management of Science Labs, Computer Rooms, Libraries, and Activity Rooms.

6. **Dedicated School Setup & Multi-Grade Governance**:
   - Institutional profile customization, multi-grade/section structure, and role-based permissions (Super Admin, Principal, Academic Coordinator, Teacher).

## 6. DATA PRIVACY, SECURITY & OWNERSHIP

1. **School Ownership of Data**: The School retains complete and exclusive ownership of all school data, teacher records, timetable databases, and student information. The Service Provider shall not claim any intellectual property over School Data.
2. **Tenant Isolation**: The School's data is isolated at the tenant/database level with strict role-based access control.
3. **Data Protection Compliance**: The Service Provider agrees to process data in full compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023** and applicable security standards. Data will be encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256).
4. **No Third-Party Sharing / Monetization**: The Service Provider strictly undertakes **never** to sell, monetize, rent, or share School Data with third-party advertisers.
5. **Data Backup & Export**: Automated daily backups are maintained. The School can export complete timetable, faculty, and attendance data in Excel/CSV/PDF formats at any time.
